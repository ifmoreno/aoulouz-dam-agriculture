Scripts included in this folder:

0. 00.get_data_GEE.ipynb  
   Downloads raster data from Google Earth Engine.

1. 01.Calculate_NDVI_Landsat_5.R  
   Computes NDVI from Landsat 5 imagery.

2. 02.1.ndvi_l5_extraction.R  
   Extracts NDVI at the parcel level (monthly and yearly).

3. 02.2.topography.R  
   Extracts topographical variables for each parcel.

4. 02.4.ndwi_precip_extraction.R  
   Extracts NDWI and precipitation (monthly and yearly).

5. 03.data_prep.R  
   Cleans and merges datasets for analysis.

6. 04.model.R  
   Runs econometric models and produces model-related figures.

7. 05.data_vis.R  
   Generates final figures for the paper.
